BACHELOR'S THESIS · BTH

Chronos-T5 vs. Mamba

Comparative analysis of foundation-model and state-space approaches for urban traffic forecasting on METR-LA.

Key findings

Chronos-T5 achieved lower MAE (1.58 mph vs. 4.57 mph), while Mamba showed faster inference (7.16 ms vs. 1.35 s) and lower KL divergence (1.07 vs. 16.45 bits). Weather covariates reduced Mamba's error by 10.57% in the evaluated setup.
